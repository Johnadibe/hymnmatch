# ACPPB Abu transcription report (hymns 225–300)

**Primary source folder:** `Hymn or Abu Snapped Pitures - last part` (128 JPEGs, `WhatsApp Image 2026-10-16 at 16.24.31.jpeg` → `16.31.10 (1).jpeg`). **Hymns 225–300 occupy the first 60 snaps**, `16.24.31` → `16.28.47` (footer pages **(185)** → **(244)**, one snap per page). The remaining 68 snaps (`16.28.52` → `16.31.10 (1)`) start at page (245) and belong to hymns **301+** (not transcribed, per instructions).
**Overlap folder:** `Hymn or Abu Snapped Pictures - second part` snaps `18.10.47` (p.185), `18.10.47 (1)` (p.186), `18.10.47 (2)` (p.187), `18.10.48` (p.188) carry the same pages as last-part `16.24.31`–`16.24.42` (hymns 225–229). They were used as a cross-check of 225–229 (title, refs, meter, scripture, stanza text); the two sets agree.
**Method:** printed hymn numbers + footer pages `(n)` + running section headers (not WhatsApp timestamps). Many last-part snaps are rotated on disk (90° or 180°) and were normalised to upright before reading; small print (scripture line, English tune line, meter) was enlarged where needed.
**Range covered:** Abu **225 → 300** (footer pages **(185)** → **(244)**; hymn 300 ends on p.(244)).
**Result:** every number 225–300 is present (**76 hymns, no numeric gaps**). **No hymn in 225–300 is partial** — each has its closing printed `Amen.` within the folder.
**Highest hymn number seen in the folder:** **400** (`16.31.09`, p.(311), under **ABỤ NKE NIGER DIOCESAN WOMEN'S GUILD**). The folder also contains Abu **392–393** (`16.31.10 (1)`, p.(305), under **ABỤ NKE NDI-IGBO CHEPUTARA**, UMU-NTAKIRI.). Hymns 301+ were **not** transcribed, and numbers between 306 and 392 were not individually checked.

Orthography note: transcribed as printed (old ACPPB spelling with apostrophe elision, e.g. `n'`, `k'`, `nk'`). Dot-under `ọ` is used throughout; a few places where `u` vs dot-under `ụ` is uncertain are listed in §2. The italic English line under each scripture quote is the printed English title (`titleEnglish`). `hymnBookRefs` is exactly as printed; empty `""` means no `(A. n)` / `(U. n)` line is printed for that hymn. `title` is the first line of the hymn. Indented continuation lines inside `lyricsIgbo` mirror the printed line indentation; `Kọrọs:—` marks a printed refrain. Verse numbers are sequential (`Verse N`).
