# ACPPB Abu transcription report (hymns 129–175)

**Source folder:** `Hymn or Abu Snapped Pictures - second part` (86 JPEGs; hymns 129–175 are on the first 41 snaps, `18.08.09` → `18.08.56`)
**Method:** printed hymn numbers + footer pages `(n)` + running section headers (not WhatsApp timestamps). Many snaps are rotated 90° on disk; each was read in its upright orientation.
**Range covered:** Abu **129 → 175** (footer pages **(103)** → **(143)**).
**Result:** every number 129–175 is present (**47 hymns, no numeric gaps**). **No hymn in 129–175 is partial** — every hymn has its closing `Amen.` (or final stanza) printed within the folder.
**Folder note:** the folder does **not** stop at 175. Numbering continues past 175 to at least **Abu 224** (footer page (184)) — see §4. The brief's expectation that "Abu 174 *Teach me Thy way, O Lord*" is the last snap is not what the images show: on `18.10.51.jpeg` that hymn is **Abu 213** (page (174), section ISO KRAIST.), and Abu 174 is actually *Jesus, my Lord, to Thee I cry* (page (142), `18.08.55.jpeg`).

Orthography note: transcribed as printed (old ACPPB spelling with apostrophe elision, e.g. `n'`, `k'`, `nk'`). Dot-under `ọ` is used throughout; `ṅ` where a dotted ṅ is printed (e.g. `ọṅu` "joy"); `ụ` is only used where a dot-under u is visible (it is rare in this book). The italic English line under each scripture quote is the printed tune/English title (`titleEnglish`). `hymnBookRefs` is exactly as printed; empty `""` means no `(A. n)` / `(U. n)` line is printed for that hymn. Indented continuation lines inside `lyricsIgbo` mirror the printed line indentation; `Kọrọs:—` marks a printed refrain (printed after verse 1 only unless stated).
